About

The short version

I work the middle of the supply chain — the part between "the forecast says" and "the truck actually showed up." At XPO I build the SQL and Python behind demand and replenishment calls, and the Power BI dashboards that let planners see fill rate and OTIF without asking me for a pull.

Before that, at DHL Supply Chain, I sat inside SAP S/4HANA doing material and supply-demand planning, running RFQ and spend analysis that shaved real cost out of sourcing. Earlier still, at Flipkart, I was closer to the floor — inbound and outbound coordination, 3PL scorecards, the operational end of what a dashboard later summarizes.

Lean and Six Sigma discipline underneath all of it. I hold a Master's in Computer Science from Texas Tech, which is mostly why the analytics side comes as naturally as the operations side.

"A forecast is only as good as the exception report that catches when it's wrong."
